How to cook Thit Kho Tau perfectly

Description

A classic Asian dish that not just very easy to make, but the flavor is phenomenal.Today, we're going to show you how to master it.

Ingredients

  • 5 eggs
  • 500g of porkchop
  • Onion
  • Chillies
  • Sugar
  • A peticular skill: Wok tossing(optional)

Steps

  1. Turn on high heat
  2. Start adding sugar in
  3. Make sure the sugar not cooking too fast, continously mixing, until they give a golden brown liquid
  4. Then quickly, add porkchop and right away, flip evenly, making sure the meat was cook perfectly on every side.
  5. Add onions, chillies, and 200ml of water
  6. Now put on a lit and let it simmer for at least 20 minutes at low heat
  7. After that, serve and enjoy!
