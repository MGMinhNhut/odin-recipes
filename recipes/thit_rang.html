How to cook Thit Rang perfectly

Description

A classic Asian dish that not just very easy to make, but the flavor is phenomenal.Today, we're going to show you how to master it.

Ingredients

  • 500g of porkchop
  • fish sauce
  • Sugar
  • Chillies
  • A peticular skill: Wok tossing(optional)

Steps

  1. Turn on high heat
  2. Start adding the porkchops in
  3. Make sure the eggs not cooking too fast and tossing them regularly at medium heat
  4. When they turn golden, start adding fish sauce and chillies and sugar
  5. Continue tossing until it's ready
  6. Now present on a dish and enjoy!
