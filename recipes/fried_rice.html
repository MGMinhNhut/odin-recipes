How to cook Fried Rice perfectly

Description

A classic Asian dish that not just very easy to make, but the flavor is phenomenal.Today, we're going to show you how to master it.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • Leftover rice
  • Onion
  • Chillies
  • A peticular skill: Wok tossing(optional)

Steps

  1. Turn on high heat
  2. Start adding eggs in
  3. Make sure the eggs not cooking too fast, then add leftover rice
  4. Tossing skillfully
  5. Add onions, chillies, pepper, spices of your choice
  6. Continue tossing until it's ready
  7. Now present on a dish and enjoy!
